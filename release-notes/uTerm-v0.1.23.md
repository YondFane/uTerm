## 中文

- 点击侧栏分组标题的任意位置即可展开或折叠，右侧操作按钮仍独立执行。
- 在底部“+”菜单或欢迎页添加 SSH、Redis、数据库连接；各添加入口跟随对应区域的显示开关。
- 窄侧栏中的连接添加菜单会完整换行显示标签，欢迎页在小窗口中自动重排并支持滚动。
- 新增中英文[官网](https://yondfane.github.io/uTerm/)，可查看功能演示、按平台下载安装包或复制一键安装命令。

## English

- Click anywhere on a sidebar section heading to expand or collapse it. The buttons on the right still act independently.
- Add SSH, Redis and database connections from the footer + menu or welcome page. Each entry follows its section's visibility switch.
- Connection add menus wrap labels in narrow sidebars, and the welcome page reflows and scrolls in smaller windows.
- Visit the new bilingual [website](https://yondfane.github.io/uTerm/) for feature demos, platform installers and one-line installation commands.
