# uTerm 0.1.35

## 中文

- 修复任务持续输出时拖动左侧栏和工具面板分隔线的卡顿。拖动可调整宽度，松手保留最终尺寸；拖至边缘收起、重新展开和键盘调整保持可用。

## English

- Sidebar and inspector dividers respond more smoothly while tasks produce output. Drag to resize
  and release to keep the final width; edge collapse, reopening and keyboard resizing remain
  available.
