## 中文

- 在“设置 → 外观 → 侧栏样式”选择经典侧栏或图标导航侧栏。图标导航栏支持按项目与会话、SSH、Redis 和数据库筛选列表，收起列表后仍保留导航及底部按钮；点击分类图标或向右拖拽可重新展开。
- 在“设置 → 外观 → 图标高亮”选择跟随主题或自定义颜色，用于已显示的连接开关、当前侧栏导航和工具面板图标。设置即时生效并在重启后保留。
- 拖窄侧栏时，标题保持单行，操作与底部按钮紧凑排列；更窄时显示资源图标，悬停可查看完整名称，菜单仍保持可读宽度。
- 添加按钮位于图标导航栏底部。“新建终端”排在添加菜单首项，SSH、Redis 和数据库连接入口依次位于末尾；经典侧栏使用相同菜单顺序。

## English

- Choose Classic sidebar or Icon navigation sidebar in Settings → Appearance → Sidebar style. The
  navigation rail filters projects and sessions, SSH, Redis and databases. Collapsing the list keeps
  navigation and footer buttons available; click a category or drag right to reopen it.
- Choose Follow theme or Custom color in Settings → Appearance → Icon highlight for visible
  connection switches, the selected sidebar category and inspector tool. Changes apply immediately
  and persist after restart.
- Narrow sidebars keep headings on one line and arrange actions and footer buttons compactly. At
  smaller widths, resource icons retain full-name tooltips and menus remain readable.
- Add sits at the bottom of the navigation rail. New terminal is first in the Add menu, with SSH,
  Redis and database connection actions at the end. The Classic sidebar uses the same menu order.
