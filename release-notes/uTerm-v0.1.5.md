## 中文

- **活动项目树**：当前工作空间中有未关闭会话的项目集中显示在侧栏“活动”区域，展开即可查看和切换终端、Agent 会话及 Worktree。关闭项目的最后一个会话后，项目回到原来的列表位置。
- **交换左右窗格**：鼠标悬浮到主工作区与工具面板之间的分隔线，点击垂直中间出现的交换按钮，即可互换终端/编辑器与文件、Git 等工具面板的位置。布局会自动记住；也可在命令面板执行“交换左右窗格”，或在设置 → 快捷键中配置按键。

## English

- **Active project tree**: Projects with open sessions in the current workspace appear together in the sidebar’s Active section. Expand a project to browse and switch between terminals, Agent sessions and Worktrees. Closing its last session returns the project to its original list position.
- **Swap left and right panes**: Hover over the divider between the main workspace and inspector, then click the swap button at its vertical midpoint to exchange the terminal/editor and panels such as Files or Git. The layout is remembered automatically. You can also run “Swap left and right panes” from the command palette or assign a key in Settings → Keyboard shortcuts.
