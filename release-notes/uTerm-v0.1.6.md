## 中文

- **Agent 按需显示**：在设置 → Agent 与用量中，打开“可用”右侧的启用开关，才会显示对应的新建会话入口。Agent 默认关闭，启用状态会自动保存；关闭开关不影响已有会话。
- **紧凑侧栏标题**：工作区选择器与侧栏隐藏按钮合并到顶部同一行，并与主工作区标题对齐。点击工作区名称即可展开切换菜单。
- **侧栏收起提示**：拖动收起项目侧栏或工具面板后，对应的展开按钮会闪烁三秒，点击即可恢复面板。开启系统减少动态效果时改为静态高亮。

## English

- **Show Agents on demand**: In Settings → Agents & usage, turn on the switch beside “Available” to show that Agent’s session creation entries. Agents start disabled and your choices are saved automatically; turning a switch off preserves existing sessions.
- **Compact sidebar header**: The workspace selector and sidebar hide button now share the top row, aligned with the main workspace header. Click the workspace name to open the switching menu.
- **Panel collapse hint**: After dragging the project sidebar or inspector closed, its reopen button flashes for three seconds. Click it to restore the panel. With the system’s reduced motion setting enabled, the hint uses a steady highlight instead.
